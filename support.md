# BlinkLedger Support

Need help or want to report a problem? Email blinkledgersupport@gmail.com.

Please do not send bank statements, screenshots, or other financial documents by email. Describe what happened instead, and tell us your phone model, the app version (shown in your store listing), and the steps that led to the problem.

## Where is my data?

On your device only. BlinkLedger has no account and no server for your data, so we cannot see or restore it for you.

## How do I back up my data?

Open Settings > Backup & restore and choose Back up now. The backup is an encrypted file protected by a password you choose. Keep the password safe: it cannot be recovered. To move to a new phone, restore that file there.

## How do I export my transactions?

Settings > Export data saves a CSV file you can open in Excel or Numbers. Exporting works even after a subscription ends.

## A screenshot or PDF was not read correctly

Recognition is automatic and can make mistakes. Every imported row is shown for review before it is saved, so you can fix the amount, date, category, or description. You can also add the transaction manually.

## How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed in your store account: on iPhone, Settings > your name > Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions.

## How do I delete everything?

Settings > Delete all local data erases every ledger, transaction, photo, and automatic copy on this device. Backups and exports you saved elsewhere are not removed.
