# BlinkLedger Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

BlinkLedger is published by BlinkVector Technologies Inc. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the BlinkLedger app. The short version: your financial data stays on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. BlinkLedger has no accounts, no sign-in, and no developer-run server for your data. We do not receive, store, or have access to your transactions, balances, accounts, categories, notes, merchant names, photos, screenshots, statements, or anything else you enter or import.

We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share personal information.

## What stays on your device

- Your ledgers, transactions, accounts, budgets, categories, rules, and settings are stored in an encrypted database on your device. The key is kept in the Apple Keychain or the Android Keystore.
- Photos you attach to a transaction are stored in the app's private storage on your device and are deleted with that transaction.
- Screenshots, photos, PDFs, and CSV files you import are read on your device. Text recognition uses Apple Vision on iPhone and Google ML Kit on Android, and both run on the device. Temporary copies are deleted after processing.
- Categorization and the optional AI assistant run on your device. On supported iPhones the system's on-device Apple Intelligence may be used. Nothing you import is sent to a cloud AI service.
- Voice input is optional. If you hold the microphone button, your speech is turned into text by your phone's speech recognizer. On iPhone the app forces this to run on the device. On Android it uses recognition on the phone when the phone has it; if not, Android may send the audio to Google, which handles it under your Google account settings, and the app asks you before the first use. BlinkLedger does not record, store, or send the audio, and the text is handled like anything else you type. The app asks for microphone permission (and speech-recognition permission on iPhone) the first time.
- If you add a transaction by talking to Siri ("Add a transaction in BlinkLedger"), Siri handles what you say under Apple's terms and gives BlinkLedger only the resulting text. The app opens with the transaction filled in and saves nothing until you confirm.

## When the app uses the internet

The core features (bookkeeping, recognition, review, search, and charts) work offline. The app connects to the internet only to:

- Process subscription purchases, restore purchases, and check subscription status through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment card details.
- Download an optional on-device AI model, only if you choose to in Settings. The download comes from the public Hugging Face model host. The request contains no images, text, categories, or financial records. The host receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address, as any website does. You can cancel a download or remove a model at any time.
- Show you this policy, the terms, and the support page. These pages are also included in the app and can be read offline.

On Android, Google's ML Kit text recognition library may send anonymous performance and diagnostic data to Google. This data does not include your images, recognized text, or financial data. Google provides no setting to turn it off.

## Backups and exports

You decide whether data ever leaves the app. Exports (CSV) and backups (an encrypted file protected by a password you choose) are saved only where you tell the system to save them. If you forget a backup password we cannot recover it. Your device's own backup service (such as iCloud or Google backup) may include the app's data according to your device settings.

## Your choices and control

- Export your data at any time, including after a subscription ends.
- Delete everything on your device with Settings > Delete all local data, or by uninstalling the app. Files you exported or backed up elsewhere are not removed by this and are under your control.
- Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot access, correct, or delete it on your behalf.

## Children

BlinkLedger is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and show the new version in the app.
